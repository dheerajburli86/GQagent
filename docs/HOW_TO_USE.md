# GQuants Assistant: how to use it

It's a chat on Telegram. You talk to it like you would message a colleague.

## Asking

Just type your question, in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

- "What's the latest news on Infosys?"
- "How did the Indian market do today?"
- "Explain what a repo rate is, simply"
- "What is 18% of 2.5 lakh?"
- "Write a polite email asking a client for the pending invoice"

**Sending a file?** Tap 📎 → File and send a PDF, Excel, Word, PowerPoint or CSV.
It tells you what's in it, then you can ask anything about it, for example:
- "What's the total by sector?" (Excel)
- "Compare this with the file I sent earlier"
- "List the companies in this file and save them as our India stocks"
- "Set up daily news on every company in this sheet"
It remembers your files for a few hours, so you can keep asking follow-ups.

Want to start over on a new topic? Type **/new**.

## The four buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| 📊 **Market today** | A short summary of today's market |
| 🔔 **My updates** | Your regular updates: send one now, pause it, or remove it |
| ⭐ **Our stocks** | The team's saved stock lists |
| ❓ **How to use** | A reminder of all this |

## Regular updates

Tell it what you want and when:

- "Every morning at 9, send me news on our India stocks"
- "Every 2 hours, tell me if anything big happens with Reliance or TCS"
- "Every Monday at 10, send a summary of the week's news on Indian banks"
- "Every evening, tell me about any results or concall transcripts from our India stocks"

It replies to confirm, and then the updates arrive here on their own. It only sends
what's new. Under each update there's a **⏸ Pause** button.

To change one, just say so: "make the Reliance update daily at 6pm instead" or
"stop the banks update".

## Economy news by country

Ask about any country on the Global Indices tile, or all of them:
"What did the Bank of Japan decide?" · "Economy news this week for all our countries".
Results come from each country's central bank and statistics office first, then the press.

## Our stocks

Admins save the team's lists once, just by saying it:
"save our India stocks: HDFC Bank, Infosys, TCS, Larsen & Toubro".
After that anyone can ask "any news on our India stocks?" and updates can follow the list.

## Choosing the AI (optional)

You don't have to do anything: normally **DeepInfra** answers.
- Start with **@grok** to use Grok instead. It searches the live web, good for very recent news.
  Example: "@grok what's the latest on the yen?"
- Write **@grok @deepinfra** to have both answer and get one combined answer.
- The same works for regular updates: "@grok every morning at 9, news on Japan's economy".

## Your projects and BusyBee (team members)

Link your Telegram in BusyBee once (Settings → Telegram). Then you can ask about
anything in our projects, and give BusyBee work just by saying it:
- "What's overdue for me in BusyBee?" · "What is Asha working on this week?"
- "Assign Ravi a task: send the Q3 report, due Friday 5 pm, high priority"
- "Mark my 'Fix tile' task as done" · "Move my deadline request…" (BusyBee's usual rules apply)
- "What alerts did FinXray send today?" · "Did last night's heatmap refresh fail?"

## Good to know

- It gives facts and sources, not buy or sell advice.
- It can't read photos yet; send documents as files (📎 → File).
- In the team group, start your message with **@** and the bot's name, or reply to one
  of its messages. Otherwise it stays quiet.
